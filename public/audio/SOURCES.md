# Courtyard field recordings

Only CC0, CC BY and public-domain recordings. Licence metadata was checked with the Commons API; evidence is in `docs/qa/audio/provenance.json`. Run `node scripts/build-audio.mjs` to regenerate. AAC is preferred for Safari/iOS; Opus is the alternative. Originals and intermediate PCM stay in ignored `artifacts/audio-source/`. These are real recordings, adapted to a fictional Soviet courtyard, not recordings made there.
