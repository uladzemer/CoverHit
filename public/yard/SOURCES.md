# Yard assets

Optional licensed models live in the ignored `stock/` directory. They are not CC0
and are excluded from distribution by default. `npm run yard:stock` imports the
owner's read-only ZIPs from `assets-src/yard/envato/`; it records item titles,
source URLs, personal Envato licence, sizes and conversion warnings in
`stock/manifest.local.json` and `stock/conversion.local.json`. Fresh clones use
procedural replacements. See `docs/qa/yard3/README.md` for placement and QA.

All external assets are CC0-1.0: https://polyhaven.com/license and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/. Downloaded 2026-10-08. Texture maps are 1024px WebP conversions; ARM packs ambient occlusion (R), roughness (G), metalness (B). HDR is 2048px. Birch/poplar crowns, alpha foliage/grass atlases, bark, laundry prints, and weathering masks are deterministic local artwork.

Courtyard architecture, furniture, playground and Zhiguli-inspired vehicle are locally constructed geometry; no external models or textures besides those listed above.

The original sparse Tree Small 02 and warm Concrete Wall 007 scans were retired. Brick Wall 13 diffuse is greyscaled and lifted to pale silicate brick; its normal and ARM maps are preserved. No suitable photoreal CC0 1970s sedan was found in the reviewed catalogs, so the car uses bevelled side-profile extrusion.

Leafy Grass replaces the bare Sparse Grass scan for summer lawns; 3 m world-space tiling, a rotated secondary sample and low-frequency colour variation break repetition. Its CC0 diffuse, normal and ARM maps are preserved. The worn dirt shortcut reuses Playground Sand with an earth tint. Sandbox paint and clump alpha are deterministic local artwork. Updated 2026-10-09.
