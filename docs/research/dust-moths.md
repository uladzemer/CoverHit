# Dust and moth research — 2026-10-10

Read the architecture/core and the shared web brief before implementation.

- [Bridson, Hourihan & Nordenstam, Curl-Noise for Procedural Fluid Flow (2007)](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/docs/bridson-siggraph2007-curlnoise.pdf): the curl of a smooth vector potential gives incompressible procedural velocity. Our shader evaluates analytic gradients of three 3D value-noise potentials, with bounded time-varying displacement. This is a visual advection approximation, not a fluid integrator or obstacle simulation.
- [PBRT 4e, Phase Functions](https://www.pbr-book.org/4ed/Volume_Scattering/Phase_Functions): HG with positive g favours forward scattering. PBRT uses two outward directions; our shader uses incident propagation `-sunDir` and outgoing particle-to-camera direction, hence denominator `1+g?-2g cos(theta)`. g=.6; brightness capped, warm grey/beige base plus sky ambient, no per-particle shadow sample.
- [Kenney Smoke Particle Assets](https://opengameart.org/content/smoke-particle-assets): verified author Kenney and CC0 listing, including original archive licence. White-puff sprites become a guttered 4x4 atlas with random offset and interpolated frames. 186,200 bytes, original ZIP stays outside repository.
- [three.quarks repository](https://github.com/Alchemist0823/three.quarks): README feature lists alone understate capabilities. The locally installed exact 0.17.1 package actually exports `Noise`, `TurbulenceField`, `FrameOverLife`, force and rotation/colour/size behaviours; `soft_fragment.glsl` samples a supplied depth texture. Existing dust used those features and a carpet depth pass. Nevertheless the specified custom shader avoids both that pass and per-particle CPU updates. Dependency removed as no source imports remain.
- Species decision follows the supplied verified addendum: generic nocturnal moth with buff/cream base and muted copper distal wings; not a phototactic Tineola. Procedural lance-shaped alpha-fringed wings, lathed body, head and long antennae. Display wingspan 5.6 cm is intentionally enlarged for play readability. Actual species-sized insects would be subpixel at this camera distance.

Soft intersections use world ground height and each emission's tangent carpet plane, ordinary depth test, no depth write. They do not track every fold of a deforming carpet. Wind is sampled once per dust update at the pad and integrated into a uniform; per-moth wind is sampled at position. No additional Ammo work. Flap uses 10–14 Hz with occasional glides. Seeded 120 Hz steering combines correlated turn noise, rare bounded heavy-tail impulses, mild upward bias, soft containment/obstacle repulsion and optional weak lamp attraction. Game retains ownership of the ~4-second lifetime and score events.

No sediment decals or wing audio: omitted per addendum/resource priority. No downloads of moth meshes; procedural geometry avoids scan costs and attribution UI changes.

Verified species references: [University of Kentucky, Clothes Moths](https://entomology.mgcafe.uky.edu/ef609) confirms light avoidance; [University of Minnesota Extension, Pantry Pests](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/home-maintenance/household-insects/pantry-pests-insects-found-in-stored-food) describes pale bases and coppery distal wings. These support the generic/pantry-inspired appearance decision rather than claiming a true clothes moth flies to lamps.
