# Photos stream research, 2026-10-10

Read ARCHITECTURE.md, CORE.md and the existing 2026-10-10-web-brief.md before resuming the partial implementation. Reused every valid cached original; no photographs were downloaded again. A live Commons `imageinfo` API query reconfirmed all twelve original URLs and licences; the results are in [provenance-verification.json](../qa/photos/provenance-verification.json). The download script independently resolves missing originals through the same API, validates the licence whitelist, supplies a User-Agent, spaces requests by 1.5 seconds, and backs off on HTTP 429/503.

The orthographic [Schiguli side photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schiguli_WAS-2101_(side).jpg) is the principal side projection. Its matching [front photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Schiguli_WAS-2101_(front).jpg) supplies the grille and windscreen separately. Both are Fiver, der Hellseher, CC BY-SA 4.0, as verified through the API. The [Lada 1600 rear](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lada_1600_--_Rear.JPG) is a three-quarter photograph: separate four-corner rectification of the rear panel and glass is necessary. The [Lada outline](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lada_1200.svg) is public domain. The body is a 62-point manual silhouette with wheel arches, longitudinal subdivisions, crown and tumblehome; its scale follows the requested 2106 dimensions. These sources produce a recognisable hybrid Zhiguli rather than a historically exact single photographed 2106. Reference-only car views are delivered with provenance but are never uploaded to the GPU.

Five facade originals supply weathered render/panel, brick with balconies, loggia stripes, tower panels and another five-storey wall. The full list, original authors and per-file licences are in [SOURCES.md](../../public/yard/SOURCES.md) and the photo manifest. Crop hints were retained from the previous run; processing now samples the full-resolution originals instead of the inspection thumbnails. The source's exact architectural series is not inferred solely from visual similarity: series identifiers select game materials, not historical certification of every source building.

[Concrete Slab Wall 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_slab_wall_02) is by Amal Kumar under CC0, reconfirmed on the source page. Its seam reference is delivered without a second runtime texture layer. Adding another scanned layer would duplicate facade detail and consume the texture budget.

[Three's texture documentation](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/Texture.html) confirms independent S/T wrapping and texture-coordinate scaling. Horizontal mirrored repeat joins the crop edges; vertical repeat remains upright so balconies and window sills do not flip every third floor. Eight edge texels of wall colour/roughness are joined at vertical boundaries. Box-face UVs use 2.7 m floors and 3.0–3.2 m bays, baked before the existing static-batch merge.

Window detection combines manually bounded glass rectangles, luminance threshold and neighbour cleanup. Extraction happens offline; runtime uses the derived mask plus a seeded cell hash to select approximately 20% of cells and tint their curtains. No additional window quads or physics objects are required. Albedo is divided by blurred luminance with bounded gain and highlights capped at 235 before WebP encoding; low-strength Sobel normals and roughness are generated offline. Near housing gets those detail maps; distant twelve-triangle box impostors share photo albedo/window maps, avoiding a separate expensive bake or render target.

Decisions: use cached licensed sources; keep car wheels/hardware procedural; share two car draws across three paint tints; retain the existing car/window geometry as failure fallback; use 1024×512 facade albedo and 256×256 auxiliary maps; preserve entrances, roof props and rain streaks; hide duplicate windows and photographed brick balconies after successful loading. Physics, UI, laundry, moths and dust remain outside this stream.
